Straight Answers

What Bob Charges

Minimum Visit
$150

Covers getting to you, getting set up, and the first hour of work.

After the First Hour
$65/hr

Straight hourly. Materials are billed at cost on top.

Full Day  (8 hours)
$550

A full 8-hour day on site. Past 8 hours, it goes back to $65/hr.

There's no trip fee on top of this. The $150 minimum only shows up when the job is small — if the work adds up to more than $150, that's simply what you pay. No surprises, no separate charge for showing up.

Bigger project — a deck, a bathroom, anything running more than a day? That's not an hourly job. Bob comes out, looks it over, and gives you a written price for the whole thing. The estimate is free, anywhere in his service area.

What that rate already covers

It isn't just an hour of labor. Already in the number:

  • His truck and his gas — both directions
  • Every tool he owns — you're not renting anything
  • A parts run if the job needs something he doesn't have on the truck
  • Cleanup when he's done
  • Hauling off what's left — the old door, the trim, the scrap. It leaves in his truck, not in your garage
